# Zero-Ad-Spend SEO Acquisition & Conversion Blueprint

## Core Strategy: Template-to-SaaS Funnel
Office managers rarely search for abstract "compliance SaaS." They search Google for **free checklists and Excel spreadsheets** when preparing for an audit or onboarding a new hygienist.
1. Capture the search via static, fast-loading SSG pages offering an immediate free CSV/Excel download (`dental_license_tracker_template.csv`).
2. Embed a prominent comparison banner right above the download button:
   > *"Spreadsheets don't send emails when a hygienist's CPR card expires next Tuesday. Automate reminders for your entire clinic for $39/mo — Start 14-Day Free Trial."*
